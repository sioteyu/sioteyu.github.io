2. Information we collect

Account and profile information

Name, email address, phone number, authentication credentials or provider identifiers, profile photo, birthday, language, country, time zone, currency, preferences, and account settings.

Household and membership information

Household name and photo, address or general location, household type, invitations, family or household relationships, member roles, permissions, availability, and activity history.

Content you and other household members provide

This may include:

  • tasks, chores, assignments, comments, rewards, and completion evidence;
  • family events, calendars, RSVPs, guest details, locations, travel plans, polls, budgets, discussions, tickets, and photo albums;
  • bills, subscriptions, amounts, due dates, receipts, payment references, and expense-sharing records;
  • shopping lists, pantry information, product preferences, and purchase history;
  • documents, identification records, certificates, contracts, warranties, school records, insurance records, and files you upload;
  • property, appliance, vehicle, repair, maintenance, and service-provider records;
  • pet, child, school, elderly-care, medication, allergy, mobility, dietary, and caregiving information;
  • emergency contacts, emergency plans, family check-ins, access instructions, and selected offline emergency information;
  • announcements, chats, notes, reactions, reports, contacts, tags, and search data.

Device, technical, and usage information

Device and app version, operating system, language, time zone, IP address, authentication and security events, crash information, performance diagnostics, notification tokens, and basic interaction logs. We use this data to operate, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the Service.

Permissions and connected services

With your permission, LifeHub may access selected photos, camera, files, contacts, calendar, notifications, biometric authentication status, or location. You can control most permissions in your device settings. Calendar, contact, or device integrations only operate when enabled by you.

Purchase information

If paid plans are offered through Apple or Google, the applicable app store processes payment-card information. We may receive purchase status, product, country, transaction reference, renewal, and cancellation information, but we do not receive your full payment-card number.

Information provided by another person

A household administrator or member may invite you, create a managed child or dependent profile, assign tasks, add emergency contacts, or enter information relating to you. The person providing that information must have a lawful basis and appropriate authority to do so.

4. Legal bases for processing

Where laws such as the GDPR or UK GDPR apply, we rely on one or more of the following:

  • Contract: processing needed to provide the Service you request.
  • Consent: optional permissions, integrations, certain notifications, and sensitive information you choose to provide.
  • Legitimate interests: securing, maintaining, supporting, and improving the Service, where those interests are not overridden by your rights.
  • Legal obligation: compliance with laws, valid legal process, tax, accounting, fraud-prevention, or safety duties.
  • Vital interests: limited situations involving protection of life or safety, where legally permitted.

5. How information is shared

With household members and invited users

LifeHub is collaborative. Information is shared according to the household, record, role, and visibility settings selected by users. Household owners and administrators may have broader access than other members. Event guests, caregivers, children, and temporary guests should receive only the access configured for them.

With service providers

We may use providers that host data, authenticate users, deliver notifications, process subscriptions, monitor crashes, provide customer support, or protect the Service. Planned infrastructure includes Google Firebase services such as Authentication, Firestore, Cloud Storage, Cloud Functions, App Check, Cloud Messaging, and optional diagnostics. Providers may process data only for contracted purposes and under applicable safeguards.

With integrations you choose

When you connect an external calendar, device service, or other integration, information needed for that feature may be sent to or received from that provider under its own privacy terms.

For legal, safety, or security reasons

We may disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with law or valid legal process, enforce agreements, investigate abuse, prevent fraud, protect rights or safety, or respond to an emergency. We review requests and disclose only what we believe is legally required or permitted.

Business transfers

If LifeHub is involved in a merger, financing, acquisition, reorganization, or sale of assets, information may be transferred subject to this policy and applicable law. We will provide notice when required.

6. Household visibility and responsibility

Household owners and administrators control invitations, roles, and many permissions. They are responsible for inviting only authorized people, reviewing access, removing former members, and selecting appropriate visibility for sensitive records.

Deleting your account does not always delete content that belongs to a shared household or is needed by other members. Where appropriate, shared records may remain with your identity removed or may be transferred to another authorized household owner. If you are the sole household owner, you may need to delete the household or transfer ownership first.

7. Children and teens

LifeHub is designed primarily for adults managing a household. A person must be old enough to enter a binding contract in their country to create and administer an independent account. Child or teen profiles must be created, invited, and supervised by a parent, guardian, or other authorized adult.

We do not knowingly allow children under 13, or a higher minimum age where local law requires, to independently register without verifiable parental authorization. Adults must not enter a child’s information unless they have authority to do so. If you believe a child’s information was collected improperly, contact us so we can investigate and delete or restrict it.

LifeHub should not be submitted to an app store’s child-directed category unless the app, SDK selection, consent flow, and data practices have been separately reviewed for all applicable children’s privacy requirements.

9. Security

We use administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect information, including encrypted network transport, authenticated access, role-based authorization, Firebase Security Rules, secure file-storage rules, protected secrets, app integrity controls where available, logging controls, backups, and security review.

No storage or transmission system is completely secure. Keep your device, email, password, recovery methods, household invitations, and emergency PINs secure. Notify us promptly if you suspect unauthorized access.

Biometric authentication is handled by your device platform. LifeHub should receive only the success or failure of the authentication check, not your fingerprint or facial biometric template.
