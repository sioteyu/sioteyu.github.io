Delete your LifeHub account

You can request deletion of your account and associated personal data using the steps below.

Delete in the app

  1. Open LifeHub and sign in.
  2. Go to More → Settings → Account & Security.
  3. Select Delete account.
  4. Review household ownership and subscription information, then confirm your request.

If you are the sole owner of a household, you may need to transfer ownership or delete the household first. Cancelling your LifeHub account does not automatically cancel an Apple App Store or Google Play subscription.

Request deletion by email

If you cannot access the app, email us from the address associated with your LifeHub account. Use the subject “LifeHub Account Deletion Request.”

Send deletion request

What will be deleted

  • Your account profile, authentication association, and personal settings.
  • Your private records and files, subject to verification and applicable law.
  • Notification tokens and active device sessions.
  • Households you solely own, when you explicitly request household deletion and ownership requirements are resolved.

What may remain

  • Shared household records needed by other authorized household members, with your identity removed where appropriate.
  • Purchase, fraud-prevention, security, dispute, tax, or legal records we must retain.
  • Temporary residual copies in encrypted backups until normal overwrite.
Processing period: Verified deletion requests are generally completed in active systems within 30 days. Residual encrypted backups may remain for up to 90 days before normal overwrite, unless a longer period is legally required.
